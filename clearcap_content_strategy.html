Content standards for every post
  • Flesch reading score 60–70 (plain English, no jargon)
  • Primary keyword in title, first 100 words, meta description
  • Internal link to an industry page and apply CTA in every post
  • 1 inline CTA mid-article ("See what you qualify for →")
  • Author byline — builds E-E-A-T trust signals for Google

Week-by-week launch timeline

Week 1 — Fix the foundation
Repair the blog, set up tracking
Fix the blog 404 (critical — nothing else works without this). Install GA4 + Google Search Console. Set up conversion events: apply modal submit, phone tap, call clicks. Connect a blog CMS (Webflow, WordPress, or Framer). Submit sitemap to Google.
Weeks 2–3 — First content wave
Publish 4 industry blog posts
Publish one post per industry: trucking, construction, restaurant, medical. Each 1,500–2,000 words. Optimize existing 4 industry pages with keyword-rich H1s and internal links to the new posts. Submit all URLs for indexing via Search Console.
Week 4 — Lead magnet launch
Create first gated asset + email capture
Build "The Business Owner's Guide to MCA Funding" as a PDF. Add email capture gate on blog sidebar and a dedicated landing page. Set up 3-email welcome sequence in Mailchimp/Klaviyo: email 1 delivers the guide, email 2 explains MCA vs loans, email 3 pushes the apply CTA.
Month 2 — Authority mode
2 deep-dive guides + LinkedIn launch
Publish "MCA vs SBA vs Line of Credit" comparison guide (3,000 words — this will rank). Launch LinkedIn company page. Post 3x/week: pull stats and insights from your blog posts into carousels. Reach out to 5 finance and trucking/restaurant publications for guest post opportunities.
Month 2 continued
Build 5 state landing pages
Launch geo-targeted pages for TX, FL, NY, GA, CA — your likely highest-volume states. Each page targets "[state] business funding fast" and links to your industry pages. This creates local SEO lift with minimal content effort.
Month 3 — Optimize and scale
Audit, A/B test, and expand distribution
Review GA4: which posts drive most apply clicks? Double down on those topics. A/B test 2 CTA variants on top blog pages. Expand state pages to 10 total. Publish 2 case studies based on Roberto, Tanya, and Darnell. Launch monthly newsletter to your growing email list.
Month 3 continued
Outreach and backlink campaign
Target 3 guest posts published on industry blogs (trucking associations, restaurant biz publications, construction trade sites). Each links back to Clear Capital. Pitch your "State of Small Business Funding" report to finance journalists for earned media coverage.

Promotion channels — matched to Clear Capital's audience

LinkedIn (priority #1)

Your decision-makers live here

CFOs, construction owners, medical supply directors — all on LinkedIn. Post 3x/week: pull a key stat from your blog ("60% of construction businesses have a 60-day payment gap"), add a short insight, and link to the full post. Tag your industries. Use polls ("What's your biggest cash flow challenge?") to drive comments and reach.

  • Carousel posts from guides perform best for B2B financial content
  • Founder-voice posts ("why we started Clear Capital") outperform brand posts
Email (lead nurture)

3-sequence drip

  • Email 1 (day 0): Deliver the guide + quick win tip
  • Email 2 (day 3): MCA vs loan explainer — position Clear Capital
  • Email 3 (day 7): Social proof (Roberto/Tanya/Darnell stories) + apply CTA
  • Weekly newsletter: 1 insight, 1 industry stat, 1 link to new blog post
Outreach & links

Backlink targets
